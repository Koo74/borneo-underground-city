## 🎯 Why Underground Cities?

Underground cities offer compelling advantages in the 21st century:

| Factor | Advantage |
|---|---|
| **Climate Resilience** | Complete protection from extreme weather, heat waves, storms |
| **Land Preservation** | Surface remains for forests, agriculture, wildlife |
| **Energy Efficiency** | Stable ~20°C year-round (no heating/cooling needed) |
| **Water Security** | Protected from droughts and contamination |
| **Safety** | Protection from natural disasters, pandemics, conflicts |
| **Space Efficiency** | Multiple layers possible — 10x surface area per km² |

**Borneo is ideal** for underground cities due to:
- ✅ **Stable geology** — no major fault lines
- ✅ **Low population density** — space for large-scale projects
- ✅ **High elevation options** — above sea-level rise projections
- ✅ **Existing infrastructure** — airports, ports, power grid
